## Export status

The image tool produced concept preview sizes despite requests for 2560 × 1440. These files are **not final YouTube upload exports**. After selecting a direction, prepare a 2560 × 1440 version with the title and motto checked in the center mobile crop. Options 2 and 3 need background extension to reach the 16:9 upload canvas without stretching the lettering.

[YouTube’s official banner guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en) recommends 2560 × 1440, requires at least 2048 × 1152 at 16:9, gives a centered 1235 × 338 text/logo safe area at minimum size, and limits files to 6 MB.
