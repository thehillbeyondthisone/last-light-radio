# The Last Lamp

PROVISIONAL DESIGN REVIEW. No footage generated.

An isolated caretaker repairs a failing lamp; only at the end do we understand someone outside needed it.

## S01 | frames 0..120 (exclusive out)
Establish a lamp that will not stay lit and the caretaker's concern.

Medium, locked camera, eye level
Caretaker turns the old lamp on; it flickers out.
Sound: Switch click, electrical sputter, room wind
Risk: Flicker must not become a rapid flashing effect

## S02 | frames 120..240 (exclusive out)
Reveal the spare fuse and make repair a possible choice.

Insert, same side of desk axis
Her hand opens a shallow tray, exposing one brass fuse.
Sound: Wooden tray scrape
Risk: Hand anatomy and single fuse identity

## S03 | frames 240..360 (exclusive out)
Show her choosing repair rather than leaving.

Medium close, same screen direction
She picks up the fuse and examines its intact filament.
Sound: Coat fabric, wind
Risk: Do not show replacement yet

## S04 | frames 360..480 (exclusive out)
Make the repair visible instead of magical.

Close on hands and opened lamp base
She seats the fuse in the socket.
Sound: Metal contact click
Risk: High hand/prop complexity; may require practical insert

## S05 | frames 480..600 (exclusive out)
Confirm the consequence of her action.

Return to opening setup
She turns the lamp on; warm steady light fills the desk.
Sound: Switch click, quiet hum
Risk: Maintain exact desk layout

## S06 | frames 600..720 (exclusive out)
Reveal the light is a beacon, not merely her comfort.

Wider view through the window
She sits beside the light while a distant boat passes in the dark.
Sound: Distant boat engine, wind softens
Risk: Geography and window orientation
