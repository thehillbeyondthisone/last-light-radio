The Last Lamp

An original 30-second writing demonstration. No generated footage yet.

A caretaker fixes a lamp. Only at the end do we understand who needed it.

What the director wrote

At closing time, a woman tries to light the lamp in an empty harbor office. It dies. Outside, fog erases the water. She could leave, but instead opens a drawer, finds a spare fuse and repairs it. The lamp finally holds. A ferry horn answers from the dark. This wasn't a light she needed to see by. It was a light someone else needed to come home.

Creative target: A tiny act of maintenance becomes an act of care. No speech, no explanatory title, no twist added by narration.
